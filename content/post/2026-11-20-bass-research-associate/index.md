The Experimental Oceanography group is hiring a research associate for Subproject 2.2 of the DFG research unit [BASS](https://uol.de/en/bass), "Impact of Surfactants on Wave Fields and Fluxes under varying Boundary Conditions" (TV-L E13, 75 %; 1 January 2027 to 30 June 2030). To apply, please see the advert on the University webpage: <a href="https://www.uni-hamburg.de/stellenangebote/ausschreibung.html?jobID=b05120308dc364e0c0b3f4b060fe1d97c19a8452">Research Associate for the Project "BASS" Sub-Project 2.2</a>

<hr/>

## The post

The specific goal of Subproject 2.2 is to understand and to quantify the strong impact varying boundary conditions have on the effect of surfactants on fluxes of energy, momentum, and matter across the sea-surface microlayer (SML). A wind-wave-surfactant measurement system developed during Phase I measures coupled air-water boundary layer dynamics, the evolution of millimeter-scale surface roughness features, and the influence of surfactants thereon, combining video, laser, digital imaging, and radar technologies.

The successful applicant will take part in experimental campaigns at the University's [wind-wave tank](https://www.ifm.uni-hamburg.de/en/workareas/experimental/wind-wave-tank.html), help upgrade the setup for measurements with heavy rain, natural and artificial seawater, and programmable wind-speed variations, and run wave measurements across wind speeds, mechanically generated waves, and surfactants of different concentration and visco-elastic properties. She/he will prepare and conduct dedicated experiments, and be responsible for the data analyses and the dissemination of the results.

More on Subproject 2.2: [BASS II at the Institute of Oceanography](https://www.ifm.uni-hamburg.de/en/workareas/experimental/projects/bassii.html).

## Your profile

University degree in physics, physical oceanography, or a related field; basic knowledge of air-sea interactions, the growth of wind waves and their damping by surfactants; practical skills; experience in laboratory experiments; ability and willingness to perform laboratory research in an international team; programming skills (e.g. Python, Matlab, Labview).
